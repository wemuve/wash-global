## Publication checklist
- Have a qualified solar professional review any manufacturer-specific or electrical statements.
- Include useful original checklists or examples, not repetitive promotional filler.
- Confirm each photo depicts its stated subject and has permission for publication.
- Add a distinct title, description, self-referencing canonical, Article and BreadcrumbList data.
- Link from relevant service sections and include the released page in the sitemap.
- Check mobile reading, contact actions and indexability after publication.
